## Definition

SHARK Movement Cycles describe the minimum continuous movement principle of HACS.

The objective is not maximum productivity.

The objective is maintaining continuous system viability.

---

## Engineering Problem

Most cognitive systems fail after complete interruption.

Failure is usually caused not by overload but by loss of operational continuity.

Long periods without execution dramatically increase activation energy.

Eventually the system stops initiating action.

---

## Engineering Solution

Instead of maximizing output,

HACS minimizes interruption.

The system always performs at least one meaningful movement.

Movement may be extremely small.

Movement must never become zero.

---

## Principle

Maximum intensity is optional.

Continuous movement is mandatory.

---

## Biological Analogy

A shark survives because movement never completely stops.

The principle is not speed.

The principle is continuity.

Likewise,

the Architect maintains operational readiness through uninterrupted low-level execution.
