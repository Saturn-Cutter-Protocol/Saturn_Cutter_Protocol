# Artifact 010 — Documentation Load / Architect Addiction

## Observation
As H-ACS expanded, the system designed to reduce cognitive load began generating additional meta-work: documentation, reductions, protocols, and repeated repackaging of substantially similar material.

## Finding
Increased methodological volume is not evidence of increased system effectiveness.

## Conclusion
New documents and protocols are not results by themselves. Priority remains testing existing mechanisms and reducing parasitic meta-work. Documentation must not substitute for external life or execution.

## Source
August 2026 Claude audit and longitudinal H-ACS record.
