# Artifact 012 — Biology as Foundation Layer

## Observation
The August audit identified sleep and biological recovery as the foundational layer for sustainable cognitive performance. The current working target is approximately 22:30–07:30 with objective sleep tracking and sustained regularity. Eight hours is treated as a working target rather than a universal physiological boundary.

## Finding
Biological recovery is a prerequisite for stable operational performance.

## Conclusion
Sleep and recovery remain foundation-layer variables. Higher-order protocols must not be built by systematically consuming the recovery required to operate them.
