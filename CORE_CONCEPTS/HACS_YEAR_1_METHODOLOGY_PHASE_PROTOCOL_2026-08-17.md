# H-ACS — Year 1 Methodology Phase Protocol

**Date:** 2026-08-17
**Phase:** YEAR 1 — META / LONGITUDINAL DEVELOPMENT
**Status:** Historical methodological checkpoint

## Purpose

This document records the methodological position established after the first independent audit of the public H-ACS repositories and the subsequent correction of the audit scope.

The purpose is not to defend H-ACS against criticism. It is to distinguish what the audit has actually established from what remains an open research question.

## 1. Current phase

H-ACS is currently in a formation and longitudinal research phase.

The primary object of study is the creator's own cognitive and behavioral system over time, under real-life load and changing conditions.

The first-year objective is to accumulate longitudinal evidence sufficient to distinguish durable patterns from temporary states, attractive language, symbolic interpretations, and methodological hypotheses.

The first-year checkpoint is **14 December 2026**.

This checkpoint is not a claim that H-ACS will be proven by that date. It is the first deliberately chosen boundary for reviewing one full year of the current behavioral architecture.

## 2. Repetition across documents

The audit correctly identified substantial overlap among four public descriptions of the same three-loop core.

However, overlap is not itself proof that the repetition is useless.

During methodology formation, one principle may legitimately be represented through different interfaces:

- engineering specification;
- architecture;
- academic framing;
- operational explanation.

The research question is partly which representation will be most effective for a future reader or operator.

Therefore the current status is:

> **Conceptual overlap detected; redundancy not yet proven.**

A later canonical-source decision may be made after sufficient evidence exists about actual operator use.

## 3. Research complexity versus operating overhead

A high cognitive cost of the current system does not automatically demonstrate architectural failure.

The research object itself is unusually large: longitudinal logs, professional work, finances, family responsibilities, health-related stress, behavioral restrictions, multiple AI roles, external memory, crisis navigation, dreams, cultural material, protocols, artifacts, and methodological revisions.

Therefore two separate variables must be distinguished:

**Research complexity** — the breadth and depth of the phenomenon being observed.

**Operating overhead** — the effort required to store, classify, retrieve, compare, and maintain the research.

The engineering objective is:

> **Reduce operating overhead without prematurely reducing research depth.**

## 4. META and EXEC

H-ACS explicitly preserves two layers.

### META

What was observed, understood, hypothesized, interpreted, or formulated inside the research process.

### EXEC

What changed outside the conversation as a consequence of a conclusion.

An EXEC consequence is not required for every session.

During Year 1, META is expected to remain the dominant research layer.

EXEC is recorded whenever an insight produces an observable external consequence: behavior, decision, protocol change, completed action, external artifact, financial result, professional result, or another measurable change.

## 5. Evidence status

H-ACS uses the following evidence hierarchy:

**Observation** — directly recorded fact.

**Hypothesis** — proposed explanation or model.

**Working Rule** — rule adopted for current operation.

**Operationally Tested** — repeated application under real conditions.

**Validated** — predefined validation criterion exists and the observed result satisfies it.

**Externalized** — the mechanism has been used or interpreted independently outside the creator-AI system.

No claim receives a higher status merely because it is repeated, elegant, intuitively convincing, or present in multiple documents.

## 6. Year-1 KPI

A central Year-1 KPI is **System Continuity**.

This refers to:

- continuity of the longitudinal record;
- continuity of the principal behavioral restrictions;
- preservation of the operating subject under real-life load;
- continued observation without destructive collapse;
- continuation of the research process itself.

This KPI is evidence about the first experimental subject. It is not evidence of universal reproducibility.

## 7. Audit function

The independent audit layer is responsible for:

- preserving evidence;
- detecting contradictions;
- separating fact from inference;
- identifying methodological drift;
- detecting premature validation claims;
- checking chronology and provenance;
- identifying repetition without assuming that repetition is already useless;
- preserving weak signals that may later prove significant.

The audit function is not required to redesign H-ACS during Year 1.

Its governing rule is:

> **Preserve evidence before optimizing architecture.**

## 8. High-stakes audit gate

For high-stakes interpretations, the preferred chain is:

`Source → Observation → Inference → Alternative explanation → Uncertainty → Evidence Status`

Symbolic or Jungian material may remain in META as interpretive material. It must not silently become engineering fact.

## 9. Year 2 transition

After the 14.12.2026 checkpoint, the project may move toward:

`Longitudinal evidence → Stable patterns → Protocol candidates → Engineering formalization → Operational tests`

Only then should aggressive compression, canonicalization, and simplification become primary engineering goals.

## 10. External reproducibility

A later testing phase may involve approximately 1–3 external participants with meaningfully different starting configurations.

Each participant should have:

`Baseline → Protocol → Fixed observation period → Adherence → Observed changes → Failure points → Independent feedback → Post-test assessment`

Only after such a phase can H-ACS make serious claims about transferability to other operators.

## 11. Core principle of the present phase

> **Do not optimize the methodology before the methodology has had enough time to reveal itself.**

The Year-1 sequence is therefore:

`Preserve → Distinguish → Audit → Observe → Hypothesize → Record`

The later sequence is:

`Test → Formalize → Compress where justified → Externalize`

## Historical note

This protocol records a correction to the initial external-audit interpretation. The audit findings remain part of the evidence base; this document records the methodological scope within which those findings should be interpreted.
