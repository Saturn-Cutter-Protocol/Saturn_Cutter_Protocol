# H-ACS Evidence Status Protocol v1.0

**Status:** Operational protocol introduced 18 August 2026  
**Scope:** H-ACS / HACS longitudinal development archive

## 1. Purpose

This protocol separates what H-ACS observes, proposes, tests, validates, and externalizes.

The purpose is not to reduce the amount of research prematurely. H-ACS is currently in an early longitudinal development phase. The primary research object is the sustained reconstruction and observation of one human cognitive-operational system under changing real-world conditions.

The protocol prevents premature promotion of hypotheses or interpretations into validated methodology.

## 2. Evidence states

### OBSERVATION

A fact, event, measurement, or recorded behavioral sequence directly present in the primary record.

No causal claim is required.

### HYPOTHESIS

An interpretation or proposed mechanism derived from observations.

A hypothesis may be useful without being true. It remains explicitly provisional.

### WORKING RULE

A hypothesis or procedure that has been consciously adopted for operational use.

A working rule is not yet validated merely because it is used consistently.

### OPERATIONALLY TESTED

A working rule that has been executed repeatedly in real conditions with recorded outcomes sufficient to evaluate whether the mechanism behaves as expected.

The record must identify the context, the intervention, and the observed result.

### VALIDATED

A claim or protocol may be called validated only when a predefined validation criterion exists and the available evidence satisfies it.

Validation must specify:

- What was being tested;
- What outcome would count as success;
- What outcome would count as failure;
- Over what observation period;
- How the result was recorded;
- What evidence supports the conclusion.

A statement must not receive `VALIDATED` status merely because it appears plausible, coherent, or repeatedly discussed.

### EXTERNALIZED

A protocol or methodological element that has been transferred beyond the creator and can be interpreted and used by another person without continuous explanation from the creator.

Externalization is distinct from validation. A protocol may be externally readable without being validated, and a validated personal procedure may remain externally untested.

## 3. Development-stage rule

H-ACS is currently in a longitudinal development stage.

The first priority is not to prove that the methodology already works for everyone. The first priority is to establish a sufficiently long, auditable development record from which stronger conclusions can later be drawn.

The initial major checkpoint is **14 December 2026**, representing approximately one year from the beginning of the current Oath-based behavioral continuity period.

A later 1–2 year observation horizon may be required before claims about broader reproducibility are warranted.

## 4. Core continuity KPI

For this development stage, the principal system-level KPI is **structural continuity of the operator**.

This means maintaining the core behavioral architecture across changing external conditions rather than demonstrating a particular emotional state or producing a particular short-term outcome.

Relevant continuity dimensions may include, where directly documented:

- continuity of the Oath / fast-dopamine abstinence protocol;
- continuity of cycle recording;
- continuity of external fixation;
- continuity of the SATURN operational switch;
- continuity of deliberate action under pressure;
- absence of substitution into a new compensatory destructive behavior.

This KPI is evidence of system persistence. It is **not by itself evidence that H-ACS is universally effective**.

## 5. Measurement discipline

Self-report is acceptable for longitudinal personal research when it is clearly identified as self-report.

Self-report must not be presented as independent validation.

Where possible, future records should preserve contemporaneous evidence rather than reconstructing scores retrospectively.

The Cycle Table remains a primary longitudinal instrument. It should remain simple and should not be replaced by additional layers unless a concrete failure requires them.

## 6. Artifact status requirement

Every new H-ACS artifact must state its current evidence status.

The minimum header is:

`Status: Observation | Hypothesis | Working Rule | Operationally Tested | Validated | Externalized`

The status must reflect evidence available at the moment of publication.

Later evidence may promote, demote, revise, or retire an artifact.

## 7. Audit gate for high-stakes claims

Before publishing a claim as methodological fact, ask:

1. What was directly observed?
2. What is inferred from the observation?
3. What was actually executed?
4. What evidence was recorded contemporaneously?
5. What result would falsify the claim?
6. Has another person used the protocol?
7. Is the current evidence sufficient for the proposed status?

If these questions cannot be answered, the claim remains provisional.

## 8. Separation of development and public proof

Internal development may be broader, messier, and more experimental than the public engineering layer.

The public repository should expose the current evidence state without presenting developmental language as established fact.

The longitudinal archive remains valuable even when a hypothesis fails. Failed hypotheses, corrected interpretations, and rejected formulations are part of the research record.

## 9. Rule of architectural economy

Developmental complexity is not itself evidence of methodological value.

New layers, names, diagrams, and documents are justified only when they reduce ambiguity, improve execution, enable measurement, preserve history, or support external use.

The objective is not maximum conceptual depth.

The objective is an architecture whose complexity is justified by demonstrated function.

## 10. Current research position

H-ACS should currently be described as a **working longitudinal human–AI cognitive-operational research system and personal methodology under development**, not as a universally validated methodology.

The strongest present evidence is the sustained longitudinal record, the repeated operational use of selected protocols, and the observed behavioral continuity under changing conditions.

The next development objective is not another theoretical expansion. It is improved evidence discipline and gradual external testing when the underlying system is mature enough to be tested.
