For these reasons, this report does **not** infer completion rates, focus ratios, discipline percentages, or causal effects from the table. Producing such metrics without a consistent denominator and measurement rule would create false precision.

The evidence supports a qualitative month classification and an artifact inventory. It does not support a quantified claim of improvement.

The relevant distinction is between:

- **activity** — something was done;
- **artifact** — a durable record or document was produced;
- **external outcome** — a concrete result occurred;
- **validated system change** — evidence shows that the system became more effective or stable.

September contains activity and documentation outputs. The available record does not establish a positive external outcome or a validated system-level improvement.

## 6. Reporting Standard for Future Months

Use the path `reports/YYYY-MM_monthly_report.md` and preserve the same sections each month:

1. Executive assessment and explicit month classification.
2. System-level findings: positive change, neutral continuity, or failure — with the operator's assessment clearly distinguished from measured evidence.
3. Artifact register with date, title, status, repository path, and evidence limits.
4. Material outcomes, kept separate from activity and documentation output.
5. Evidence quality and measurement limitations.
6. Open cycles and next-month control points.
7. Privacy review before publication.

Each artifact should have one explicit status: **Draft**, **Observation**, **Working Hypothesis**, **Operationally Tested**, or **Published**. Do not label a hypothesis as a validated rule or a document as a material outcome.

For the Cycle Table, the minimum fields needed for reliable monthly reporting are: event date, logging date, activity, outcome, status, artifact title/path, evidence level, and whether the entry concerns a core Vow or a separate local control issue.
